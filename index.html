Bryson Loflin

I’m Bryson Loflin, a 2nd-year PhD student in Gerry Carter’s lab at Princeton studying friendships in vampire bats. I am interested in how those relationships are formed, maintained, and even lost. This website is meant to showcase who I am and the projects I’m currently working on. Always feel free to reach out via email with questions, comments, and even potential collaborations!
